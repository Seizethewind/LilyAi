# taste-skill 07: report and never
use when: step 07 of taste-skill, the last one, after the checks.

## Report
Write one report with:
- the kind;
- the deal: palette, pairing, hero, grounds, effect, moments, scene (or "dealt by hand");
- the checker, layout and perf results;
- what you could not check;
- every assumed price, hour or address, and the search for the coined name;
- the photographs and art the client must supply, every stand-in frame among them;
- every client slot, its file and its stand-in.

## Never
- A card, row or tile that animates on scroll; a reveal, fade-in or parallax; random blinking.
- Stock photographs passed off as the client's product, people or place; a product page that never shows the product.
- A drawn look-alike of a real logo; invented reviews, quotes, customers or figures.
- Violet or lavender in the page's own design: accent, ground, gradient, effect colour or tint (a real third-party logo keeps its own colours, `icons.md`).
- Copying a reference site's code, copy, pictures or signature effect.
- Deleting or editing the kit's own files (`assets/`, `scripts/`) to clear a check: a check that flags a kit file is reported, and the page is checked again after any change.

## Done when
- The report has every item above.
- No item in the Never list happened.
- Nothing is reported as passed unless a check actually passed.

End of taste-skill.
