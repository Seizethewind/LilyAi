# taste-skill 02: copy first
use when: step 02 of taste-skill, after the kind is chosen.

Open and read in full: `<skill>/references/copy.md` (five rules, plain versions, headlines and text, what may be assumed, contacts the brief does not give, banned words).

## Steps
1. **Write all the copy before any layout,** as `copy.md` says.
2. The h1 names the category and the buyer or the place (a game: its name and genre).
3. One hard fact on the first screen.
4. A noun in every heading.
5. Buttons are a verb and an object.
6. At most two hero actions.
7. Nothing is invented except:
   - a coined brand name, searched first, with the result kept in `names.json` beside the page;
   - on a place, plausible hours and prices.
8. No invented reviews, quotes, customers or figures.
9. On tech, 60 to 80 words a screen outside UI mocks.
10. Contacts follow house rule 9 (street and district only, `.example` contacts, no real-format phone).
11. Keep a list of every assumed price, hour or address. It goes in the report.

## Done when
- Every heading and button is written.
- The h1 and one hard fact are ready for the first screen.
- Nothing is invented beyond what step 7 allows.
- You have the list of assumed prices, hours and addresses.
